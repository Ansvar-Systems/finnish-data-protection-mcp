# Coverage

This document describes the corpus completeness and known gaps for the Finnish Data Protection MCP.

**Authority:** Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto (TSV) — Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman  
**Jurisdiction:** Finland (EU/GDPR)  
**Source:** <https://tietosuoja.fi/>

---

## Decisions (Päätökset)

**Covers:** TSV enforcement decisions including:
- Seuraamusmaksut (administrative fines)
- Päätökset (enforcement decisions)
- Huomautukset (reprimands / notices)

**Coverage start:** 2019-01-01 (decisions since GDPR entered into force)  
**Source URL:** <https://tietosuoja.fi/paatokset>

### Known Gaps
- Pre-GDPR decisions under Henkilötietolaki 523/1999 are **not** included
- Pending/draft decisions not yet published on tietosuoja.fi are not included
- Full text may be redacted where TSV has published only a summary for third-party privacy reasons

---

## Guidelines (Ohjeet ja suositukset)

**Covers:** TSV guidance including:
- Ohjeet (guidelines)
- Suositukset (recommendations)
- Kannanotot (statements / position papers)
- FAQ documents

**Coverage start:** 2018-05-25 (since GDPR implementation)  
**Source URL:** <https://tietosuoja.fi/ohjeet-ja-julkaisut>

### Known Gaps
- EDPB guidelines referenced but not co-published by TSV are not duplicated here — use the EDPB MCP for those
- Archived pre-GDPR guidance from Tietosuojalautakunta is not included
- Some documents are available in Swedish or English only and may have incomplete Finnish-language indexing
